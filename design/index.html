Medium · Two Pointers · Opposite ends

Biggest Water Tank

Vertical posts stand in a row, one unit apart, and posts[i] is the height of post i. Pick two posts and stretch a sheet between them to form a tank. The water level can't rise above the shorter post.

Return the largest amount of water (width × height) a tank can hold.

Example 1
Inputposts = [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7]
Output49
WhyPosts 1 and 8 (heights 8 and 7), 7 apart: 7 × 7.
Example 2
Inputposts = [1,1]
Output1
WhyThe only tank.
Example 3
Inputposts = [4,0,0,4]
Output12
WhyWidth 3, height 4.
Constraints
  • 2 ≤ posts.length ≤ 10⁵
  • 0 ≤ posts[i] ≤ 10⁹
