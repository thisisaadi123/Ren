You may cut one contiguous block of letters out of `s` and glue the two remaining pieces together. The block may be empty, and the kept text is a prefix of `s` followed by a suffix of `s`.

Return the length of the shortest block you can cut so that the remaining text is a palindrome.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ s.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `s` has only lowercase English letters.
