There are three pegs numbered `1`, `2` and `3`. Peg `1` holds `n` rings, largest at the bottom and smallest on top. Move them all to peg `3`. A move takes the top ring of one peg and puts it on another, and a ring may never sit on a smaller ring.

Return the moves of the shortest solution, each as `[from, to]`, in order.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ n ≤ 14`
