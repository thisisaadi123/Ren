A maze is a grid `maze` where `0` is open and `1` is a wall. Count the routes from the top-left cell to the bottom-right cell that move up, down, left or right through open cells and never visit a cell twice.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ rows, columns ≤ 5`
- The top-left and bottom-right cells are open.
