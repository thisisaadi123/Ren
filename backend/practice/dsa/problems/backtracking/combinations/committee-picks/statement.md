A club has `n` members numbered `1` to `n`, and it needs a committee of exactly `k` of them. The order in which members are chosen doesn't matter.

Return every possible committee, each written with its member numbers in **increasing** order. The committees may be returned in any order.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ n ≤ 15`
- `1 ≤ k ≤ n`
