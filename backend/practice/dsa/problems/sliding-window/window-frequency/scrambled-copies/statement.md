A **scrambled copy** of `word` is any rearrangement of its letters, including `word` itself.

Return the starting index of every substring of `text` that is a scrambled copy of `word`, in increasing order.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ text.length, word.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `text` and `word` have only lowercase English letters.
