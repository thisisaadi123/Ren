Seats around a round table are listed in `seats`: `1` is a team member and `0` is a guest. The first and last seats are next to each other.

In one swap, any two people trade seats. Return the **fewest swaps** needed so that all team members sit in one unbroken group around the table.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ seats.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `seats[i]` is `0` or `1`.
