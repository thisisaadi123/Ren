An ice-cream counter has tubs in a row, and `flavours[i]` is the flavour of tub `i`. You walk along the counter from any starting tub, taking one scoop from every tub you pass, and you must stop before you reach a tub you can't take. Your cone holds **only two flavours**, though as many scoops of each as you like.

Return the largest number of scoops you can collect.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ flavours.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `0 ≤ flavours[i] < flavours.length`
