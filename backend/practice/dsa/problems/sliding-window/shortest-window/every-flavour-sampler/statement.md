Jam jars stand on a shelf, and `jars[i]` is the flavour of jar `i`. You want to buy one stretch of neighbouring jars that includes **every flavour on the shelf** at least once.

Return the length of the shortest such stretch.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ jars.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `1 ≤ jars[i] ≤ 10⁹`
