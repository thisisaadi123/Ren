A stamp collector's album is the string `s`, with one stamp per letter: `a`, `b` or `c`. A run of consecutive stamps is a **full set** if it contains at least one of each.

Count the full sets: the substrings of `s` that contain at least one `a`, one `b` and one `c`.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `3 ≤ s.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `s` has only the letters `a`, `b` and `c`.
