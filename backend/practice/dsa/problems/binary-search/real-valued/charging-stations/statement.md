Electric-car charging stations stand along a highway at the positions `stations` (sorted, all different). You may build `extra` more stations anywhere, including at non-integer positions.

Minimise the largest distance between two neighbouring stations, and return that distance. Answers within `10⁻⁶` are accepted.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `2 ≤ stations.length ≤ 2000`
- `0 ≤ stations[i] ≤ 10⁸`, strictly increasing
- `1 ≤ extra ≤ 10⁶`
