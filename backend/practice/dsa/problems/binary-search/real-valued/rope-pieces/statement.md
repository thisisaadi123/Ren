You have ropes of lengths `ropes`. Cut them (without joining pieces) into at least `pieces` pieces that all have the **same** length. Leftover bits are thrown away.

Return the largest possible piece length. Answers within `10⁻⁶` (relative, for large values) are accepted.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ ropes.length ≤ 10⁴`
- `1 ≤ ropes[i] ≤ 10⁷`
- `1 ≤ pieces ≤ 10⁶`
