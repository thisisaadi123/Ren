A fence is made of panels of width 1, and `panels[i]` is the height of panel `i`. A poster exactly as tall as panel `i` is glued so that it covers panel `i`, and it may spread sideways over neighbouring panels as long as every panel it covers is **at least** as tall as panel `i`.

For each panel, return the widest such poster can be.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ panels.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `1 ≤ panels[i] ≤ 10⁹`
