Stones cross a river in a row, and stepping on stone `i` adds `stones[i]` points to your score; some stones subtract points. You start on stone `0` and must finish on the last stone. Each step moves you forward by **1 to `k`** stones.

Return the highest score you can finish with, counting every stone you step on, including the first and the last.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ stones.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `1 ≤ k ≤ 10⁵`
- `-10⁴ ≤ stones[i] ≤ 10⁴`
