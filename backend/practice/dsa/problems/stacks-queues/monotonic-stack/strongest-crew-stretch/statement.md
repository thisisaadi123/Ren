Workers stand in a row, and `strength[i]` is worker `i`'s strength. A crew is any non-empty run of neighbouring workers. A crew's **power** is its weakest member's strength multiplied by the sum of all its members' strengths.

Return the largest power any crew can have.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ strength.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `1 ≤ strength[i] ≤ 10⁶`
