A judge ranked contestants, and `ranks` lists the ranks in the order the contestants performed. A pair of contestants is **out of order** when the one who performed earlier got a larger rank number than the one who performed later.

Return the number of out-of-order pairs.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ ranks.length ≤ 1000`
- `1 ≤ ranks[i] ≤ 10⁹`
