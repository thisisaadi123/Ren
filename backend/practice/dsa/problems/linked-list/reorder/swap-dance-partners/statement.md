Dancers stand in a line stored as a linked list from `head`. Couples are formed from the front: the 1st and 2nd dancers, the 3rd and 4th, and so on. When the music changes, the two dancers of every couple trade places. A dancer left without a partner at the end stays where they are.

Swap every adjacent pair of **nodes** (don't just swap the values) and return the new head.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- The list has between `0` and `10⁵` nodes.
- `-10⁶ ≤ node value ≤ 10⁶`
