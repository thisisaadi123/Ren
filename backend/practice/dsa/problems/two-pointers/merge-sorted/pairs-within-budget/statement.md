A restaurant offers `mains` and `sides`, each list of prices sorted in increasing order. Count the (main, side) combinations whose total price is at most `budget`.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ mains.length, sides.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `1 ≤ mains[i], sides[j] ≤ 10⁹`, each list sorted in increasing order
- `1 ≤ budget ≤ 2 × 10⁹`
