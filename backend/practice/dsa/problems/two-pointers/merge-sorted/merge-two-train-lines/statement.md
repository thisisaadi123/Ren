Two trains are linked lists of cars, each sorted by car number in increasing order. Join them into one train sorted in increasing order, reusing the existing cars, and return its first car.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `0 ≤` cars in each train `≤ 5 × 10⁴`
- `-10⁵ ≤ Node.val ≤ 10⁵`, each train sorted in increasing order
