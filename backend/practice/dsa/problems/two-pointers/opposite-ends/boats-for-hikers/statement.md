Hikers need to cross a lake. Each boat holds **at most two** hikers, and their total weight must be at most `limit`. `weights[i]` is hiker `i`'s weight, and nobody weighs more than `limit`.

Return the fewest boats needed to carry everyone.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ weights.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `1 ≤ weights[i] ≤ limit ≤ 3 × 10⁴`
