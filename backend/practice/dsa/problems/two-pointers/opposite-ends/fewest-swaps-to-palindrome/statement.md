In one move you may swap **two neighbouring** letters of `word`. `word` can always be rearranged into a palindrome.

Return the fewest moves needed to turn it into a palindrome.

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ word.length ≤ 2000`
- `word` has lowercase English letters.
