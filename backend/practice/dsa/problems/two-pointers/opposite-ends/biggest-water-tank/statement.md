Vertical posts stand in a row, one unit apart, and `posts[i]` is the height of post `i`. Pick two posts and stretch a sheet between them to form a tank. The water level can't rise above the shorter post.

Return the largest amount of water (width × height) a tank can hold.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `2 ≤ posts.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `0 ≤ posts[i] ≤ 10⁹`
