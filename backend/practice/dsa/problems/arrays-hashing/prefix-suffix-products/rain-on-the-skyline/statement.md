A skyline is drawn as columns of width 1, and `walls[i]` is the height of column `i`. After a heavy rain, water settles in every dip between taller columns. Water flows off both ends of the skyline.

Return the total amount of water held, in unit squares.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ walls.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `0 ≤ walls[i] ≤ 10⁵`
