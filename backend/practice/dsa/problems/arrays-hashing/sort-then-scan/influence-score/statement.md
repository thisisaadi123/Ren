A researcher's **influence score** is the largest number `h` such that they have at least `h` papers with at least `h` citations each.

Given `citations[i]`, the number of citations of paper `i`, return the score.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ citations.length ≤ 10⁵`
- `0 ≤ citations[i] ≤ 10⁹`
