Runners finished a race and `order` lists their bib numbers in the order they crossed the line. The bibs are the numbers `1` to `n`, each exactly once.

In one swap you exchange the positions of any two runners. Return the fewest swaps needed to put the list in increasing order.

{{examples}}

**Constraints**
- `1 ≤ n ≤ 10⁵`, where `n = order.length`
- `order` is a permutation of `1` to `n`.
